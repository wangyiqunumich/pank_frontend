### PanKgraph eQTL and GWAS Data Sources

PanKgraph integrates expression Quantitative Trait Loci (eQTL) and Genome-Wide Association Study (GWAS) data from the following key sources:

#### eQTL Data Sources

1. **GTEx Consortium**

   The Genotype-Tissue Expression (GTEx) project provides a comprehensive atlas of genetic regulatory effects across multiple human tissues. This resource is instrumental in understanding how genetic variation influences gene expression in different tissue types. [oai_citation_attribution:3‡AAAS](https://www.science.org/doi/pdf/10.1126/science.aaz1776?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

2. **InsPIRE Consortium**

   The InsPIRE (Integrated Network and Systems Biology of Pancreatic Islet Regulation) consortium focuses on genetic variants affecting gene expression in human pancreatic islets. Their work enhances the understanding of gene regulation in pancreatic tissues, which is crucial for diabetes research. [oai_citation_attribution:2‡Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-18581-8.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

#### GWAS Data Source

1. **Type 1 Diabetes (T1D) GWAS**

   A comprehensive GWAS identified multiple genetic loci associated with Type 1 Diabetes, providing insights into the genetic underpinnings of the disease. [oai_citation_attribution:1‡Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d42859-021-00006-1?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

These datasets collectively enable PanKgraph to offer a robust platform for exploring the genetic architecture of complex traits and diseases.
