About Functional Data

The functional data shown here were generated by the Human Pancreas Analysis Program (HPAP) and the Human Islet Phenotyping Program (HIPP) of the Integrated Islet Isolation Program (IIDP) using a Vanderbilt-developed islet phenotyping pipeline shared with both HPAP and HIPP. These data capture human islet insulin or glucagon secretion in response to physiological and pharmacological stimuli over time and are displayed with PanKgraph-linked donor context. The HPAP dataset has not yet been published; the HIPP data has been published (PMID: 39605606). The underlying pipeline has been used since 2013 and is documented in prior publications, including PMID: 38049589, 35642629, 34870058, 33844836, 34428183, 32352931, 30507613, 29669939, 29514095.

Important

This tool supports interactive filtering and visualization of islet functional data. Formal statistical conclusions should be based on the corresponding analyses published in PMID: 42120367
