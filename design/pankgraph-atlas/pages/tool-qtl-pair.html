About QTL Explorer

PanKgraph QTL integrates variant-to-gene regulatory evidence to support exploration of genetic regulation in pancreatic and T1D-relevant contexts.

Important

This tool is designed for exploratory lookup and knowledge discovery.

It checks whether QTL evidence exists in PanKgraph; it does not perform new QTL mapping or causal inference.

Why use this tool?

Search regulatory evidence from either gene or variant direction

Check specific SNP-gene QTL relationships

Connect QTL signals to PanKgraph genes, variants, GWAS, pathways, and ontology annotations

Move directly from query configuration to structured QTL results
